01 About

I build Android apps in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. I theme KDE Plasma, recreating the Aero and Vista look in QML. Everything runs in Termux on the device, no root.

Most of my repositories are active forks I keep buildable, plus a few originals like pdx-bus-tracker and ghost-rain.
